Duct Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts duct sections, hangers and tape with division and rounding-up: the sections a duct run takes, the hangers along it, the tape and rolls for the joints, and the cost. It uses one named constant: 12 inches in a foot. The run, the section length, the hanger gap, the joints, the tape a joint, the roll length and the prices are yours to measure and read off your own plan and receipt. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your building and does not know your airflow, your duct size, your code, your seams or your system, and it gives no verdict that a duct run works or is sound. Every other number here is your own entry.

The sections truth

Sections: the duct run divided by the section length, rounded up, with the feet left.

The hangers truth

Hangers: the duct run over the hanger gap, rounded up, plus 1 for the far end.

The tape truth

Tape: the joints times the inches a joint, over 12 inches a foot rounded up, then divided by the roll length, rounded up, with the feet left.

The cost truth

Cost: the sections and the rolls, each at their price.

Duct Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
