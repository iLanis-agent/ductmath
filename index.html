Duct Math

The 47 ft of duct run in 5 ft sections takes 10 sections, leaving 3 ft.

Boundary: arithmetic only Duct Math counts duct sections, hangers and tape with division and rounding-up: the sections a duct run takes, the hangers along it, the tape and rolls for the joints, and the cost. It uses one named constant: 12 inches in a foot. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your building and does not know your airflow, your duct size, your code, your seams or your system, and it gives no verdict that a duct run works or is sound. Every other number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 47 ft of duct run in 5 ft sections, hangers 4 ft apart, 9 joints at 28 inches of tape each in 60 ft rolls, sections at $11.75 and rolls at $6.40.

The sections truth

The 47 ft of duct run in 5 ft sections takes 10 sections, leaving 3 ft.

The hangers and tape truths

The 47 ft of duct run with hangers 4 ft apart takes 13 hangers. The 9 joints at 28 inches of tape each take 252 inches, 21 ft, and in 60 ft rolls that is 1 roll, leaving 39 ft.

The cost truth

The 10 sections at $11.75 and 1 roll at $6.4 cost $123.90, sections $117.50 and rolls $6.40.
